Personal node terms

For the individual, this program is completely free — free to download, free to run, no account, no fee, no permission to ask. Downloadable and accessible to anyone. Individuals are the network, not its customers.

If this node is ever put to business or government use, it stops being a personal node: it must be re-declared as a business node and the business terms below must be met first. Personal terms do not carry over to business use, and there is no silent upgrade.

Business and government terms — the hard line

What is being built: a network of sovereign nodes and a vault whose floor is code-enforced backing, built only from what is fed into it, under rules locked at building. No individual — including the founder — owns the floor or can redirect what funds it.

Why this line exists: individuals built this foundation by feeding it, and individuals run it for free because they are the network. An entity with resources does not ride free on that foundation. To utilize this program, a business or government first builds its own standing the same way — it cements its foundation into the same floor and basins. That is the whole of the line: understood completely, or not used at all.

A business or government entity utilizing this program must declare its node a business node and meet these terms before use — feed the floor before you take it for a spin:

  1. Declare. The node is declared a business node in its configuration. Government entities are business class for these terms.
  2. Cement your foundation. The entity cements its own foundation with volatile digital currency passed through the converter. Converted value is divided across the basins; the floor's share backs the coin one to one.
  3. No digital currency? An agreed amount instead. An entity that holds no volatile digital currency may offer a dollar amount agreed upon by the parties involved. The agreed amount is used to purchase volatile digital currency, which then passes through the converter exactly as above — dollars themselves never enter the floor.
  4. The money funds the floor and the basins — never an individual. By either path, what enters is converted value through the converter, and it goes to the floor and the basins. It is not payment to any person.
  5. Personal use stays free. These terms are the price of business and government use only. They do not apply to individuals running personal nodes.
